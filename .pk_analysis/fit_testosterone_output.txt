1. TESTOSTERONE CYPIONATE (TC)  --  source: Bi et al. 2018, Table 2
==========================================================================
  CL/F = 2.6 kL/day, V/F = 14.4 kL, Ka = 1.22 /day
  -> disposition rate      ke = CL/V = 0.180556 /day
  -> terminal half-life       = 3.8390 d   (paper's post hoc median: 4.05 d, agreement 5.2% )
  -> absorption half-life     = 13.636 h
  -> Tmax = ln(ka/ke)/(ka-ke) = 1.8381 d
     95% CI on Ka (0.785-2.67) -> Tmax in [1.08, 2.43] d
  -> S = 1e5*Ka / (V_L * EDF * ke) = 67.13   (was 1081.0)

  Single 100 mg dose  -> Cmax 498.3 ng/dL at t=1.84 d
     cross-check vs 200 mg -> 1112 ng/dL scaled to 100 mg = 556 ng/dL  (model 498, -10.4%)

  Weekly 100 mg at steady state (exogenous contribution only):
     Cmax   735.8 ng/dL at t=1.52 d
     Cmin   320.8 ng/dL
     Cavg   549.5 ng/dL
     peak/trough ratio 2.29

  Bi basal secretion b_base=6.24 mg/d / CL -> endogenous floor 240 ng/dL
     total predicted = 976 peak / 561 trough
     Figure 2 (100 mg intensive panel) observed ~1100-1200 peak / ~400-500 late

3. ORAL TESTOSTERONE UNDECANOATE  --  source: Swerdloff 2020 (Jatenzo)
==========================================================================
  237 mg TU BID -> Cavg 488.7, Cmax 1008.3 ng/dL, Tmax 3.0 h
  EWF = 0.631  (label: 237 mg TU = 150 mg T equiv -> 0.633, agrees)
  targets: Tmax 0.1250 d, Cmax/Cavg 2.063
     feasible Cmax/Cavg given Tmax = 3 h:
       slow half-life 0.09 d ( 2.16 h) -> Cmax/Cavg 1.615
       slow half-life 0.12 d ( 2.88 h) -> Cmax/Cavg 1.580
       slow half-life 0.15 d ( 3.60 h) -> Cmax/Cavg 1.526
       slow half-life 0.20 d ( 4.80 h) -> Cmax/Cavg 1.444
       slow half-life 0.30 d ( 7.20 h) -> Cmax/Cavg 1.330
       slow half-life 0.50 d (12.00 h) -> Cmax/Cavg 1.216
       slow half-life 1.00 d (24.00 h) -> Cmax/Cavg 1.116
     target 2.063 exceeds the achievable maximum 1.615.
     The reported ratio is inflated by mean-of-maxima bias: Cmax is the
     mean of per-subject maxima (E[max] >= max[E]) and carries SD +-581
     on a mean of 1008. Anchor to Tmax and Cavg -- the label's own
     primary endpoint and the quantity clinicians titrate on -- and
     accept a documented under-prediction of peak fluctuation.
  -> slow half-life = 0.0900 d (2.16 h)
  -> fast half-life = 2.003 h
  -> Tmax = 3.00 h
  -> S = 13.57   (previously fell through to the hardcoded 12000.0)

  Steady state 237 mg BID: Cavg 489 (obs 488.7), Cmax 789 (obs 1008.3), Cmin 149
  NOTE: F is set to 1.0 because absolute oral TU bioavailability is not
        established in the source. F and S are confounded -- only their
        product is identified by these data.
