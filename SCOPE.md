# xT Project Scope Document

## 🎯 Overall Goal
To create a leading, community-backed research and support platform for individuals navigating HRT.

## 🚀 Phase 1: MVP (The Tracker App)
*   **Core Features:** Secure injection logging, customizable reminders, historical journey view.
*   **Critical Constraint:** Must adhere to strict health data privacy standards (HIPAA/GDPR consideration).

## 📈 Phase 2: Research Foundation
*   **Goal:** Make the aggregated, anonymized data useful to actual scientists.
*   **Deliverables:** A data schema, an insights report template, and educational content library.

## 🏢 Phase 3: Business Incubation
*   **Goal:** Legal viability to receive institutional grants.
*   **Milestones:** Legal entity registration (LLC/Non-Profit in Central Ohio), Business Plan v1.0.
