# ✨ xT Project Synthesis Summary

**Date Created:** June 3, 2026
**Prepared For:** Internal Development Team & Stakeholders
**Objective:** To consolidate the vision, scope, and technical requirements for the xT platform, serving as the single source of truth for development and funding pitches.

---

## 🌟 The Core Vision
To become the leading technological and research platform for Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) care, empowering users through technology while advancing medical understanding through scientific data analysis.

## 🎯 Three Pillars of Development

1.  **User Empowerment (MVP Goal):** A robust, secure mobile application that allows users to track vital personal health data (injections, symptoms, mood) with minimal friction.
2.  **Research Advancement (The Value Engine):** Transforming anonymous, aggregated user data into actionable scientific insights that can drive clinical research and improve protocols.
3.  **Business Sustainability (The Goal):** Establishing a legal entity (LLC/Non-Profit in Central Ohio) capable of securing grants to fund the research pillar.

*   **Key Data Points:** Must track $\text{Medication Type}$, $\text{Admin Method}$, $\text{Dose Date}$, and quantifiable symptoms (Mood, Skin, Body).

## 🚀 Development Roadmap (Milestones)

**Phase 1: Minimum Viable Product (MVP) - The Tracker App (Focus: Functionality)**
*   **Goal:** Launch a functional tracker for the user.
*   **Key Feature:** The 4-Step Onboarding Flow (Welcome $\rightarrow$ Profile $\rightarrow$ Medication Setup $\rightarrow$ Log First Dose).
*   **Success Metric:** User can log a dose and receive a functional reminder alert for the next cycle.

**Phase 2: Research Insights Dashboard (Focus: Value)**
*   **Goal:** Prove the research utility of the data.
*   **Key Deliverable:** A dashboard presenting statistical correlations (e.g., `Correlation Strength Score`) and longitudinal trend reports that appeal directly to a grant committee.

**Phase 3: Business Incubation (Focus: Sustainability)**
*   **Goal:** Formalize the entity.
*   **Key Tasks:** Determining the optimal legal structure (LLC vs. Non-Profit) and writing the initial grant proposal draft.
