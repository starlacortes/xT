01One curve, honestly labeled

No streaks, badges or scores. A modeled level, the labs that calibrated it, where you are now, and the range that is yours — everything else is up to you to read.

02Principles

The sovereignty of the curve

The body is a continuous rhythm, not a checklist. The modeled curve is the hero of every surface that shows a level.

Calm empirical coordinates

Measurements, ranges and rates of change — never verdicts. No “good/bad”, no status colors, no congratulation. The reader decides what a number means.

Discretion first

Nothing — icon, notification, lock-screen glance — may out a person as being on HRT. When presence and discretion conflict, discretion wins.

03What is, and what you adjust

Two kinds of content, two kinds of surface. What simply is — where your level is now — sits flush on the paper, with no card or container. What you can act on — a dose due tonight — sits in a card, one hairline deep.

04Quiet on every lock screen

A dose reminder can name nothing at all — “Reminder · Your scheduled entry is ready.” — and by default, it does. You choose every field it shows, down to writing your own words for the title.

On your home screen the same is true: the app’s icon can render as relief alone, no hue, no name — present and nearly invisible at once.
