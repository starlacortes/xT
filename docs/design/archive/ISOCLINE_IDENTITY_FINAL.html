Identity system · final · October 2026

Isocline

Final identity system — wordmark, mark, and icon set, locked from the explorer and the brand brief §7.

1 · Wordmark

ISOCLINE, all-caps, set in IBM Plex Serif Semibold — an engineered serif that shares family DNA with the brand's IBM Plex Mono. The curve sits under the word, not in it.

  • Face: IBM Plex Serif, 600, all-caps
  • Tracking: +0.07em
  • Curve: starts under the C (40% of word width), rises through L–I, plateaus under N–E, extends ~3% past the right edge
  • Marker: rose point on the plateau end — the "you are here"
  • Small sizes: the wordmark is display-only; below ~32px the ◉ mark takes over

2 · The ◉ mark

The dot-in-dot bullseye is the standalone brand mark — app icon, letterhead, favicon, lockups. Halo: lavender at 15% opacity. Inner dot: rose at 51% of the halo diameter.

The family

One mark family, three lockups. The arms differ by glyph variation — the mark tells the story the word doesn't have room for.

3 · App icon

The bullseye on a soft per-theme gradient. The icon is theme-aware: named-theme alternates (Rising / Peak / Trough / Steady lavender + sage) swap with the user's in-app theme picker; custom hues map to the nearest theme (or iOS 18 system tinting). Explicit dark variants ship on deep-indigo grounds. 10 themed assets + 1 stealth = ~11 total.

Rose and lavender lead — no competitor and no clinic portal defaults to this pair. The dark set stays in the violet family.

5 · Typography

Three faces, three jobs: serif for the wordmark and display, mono for values and measurements, a warm humanist sans for prose.

6 · Principles & carried forward

Discretion. The icon must never out a user as being on HRT at a glance — discretion beats brand presence by default. The name itself is the first discretion asset: nothing about "Isocline" reads as hormone therapy.

Umbrella

One mark family serves all three arms: the app, Isocline Research (papers), and Isocline Labs (company).

Settled decisions — recap

  • Wordmark — all-caps ISOCLINE in IBM Plex Serif; curve under the word, starting under the C, plateauing under N–E past the right edge with the marker; display-only.
  • Mark — the ◉ dot-in-dot bullseye is the standalone mark (inner dot 51% of halo, halo at 15% opacity); arms: Research = dot + halo ring, Labs = ring + right-offset dot + plateau line.
  • Icon — bullseye (dot 51%, halo 15%) on per-theme gradients; named-theme alternates swap with the in-app theme; explicit dark variants; one stealth alt.
  • Principle — discretion: the icon never outs a user as being on HRT at a glance.
  • Carried forward — landing headline direction: “Shaped protocol.”
