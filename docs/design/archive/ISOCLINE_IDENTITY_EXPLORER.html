Settled decisions — recap

  • Wordmark — all-caps ISOCLINE in IBM Plex Serif; curve under the word, starting under the C, plateauing under N–E past the right edge with the marker; display-only.
  • Mark — the ◉ dot-in-dot bullseye is the standalone mark (inner dot 51% of halo, halo at 15% opacity); arms: Research = dot + halo ring, Labs = ring + right-offset dot + plateau line.
  • Icon — bullseye (dot 51%, halo 15%) on per-theme gradients; named-theme alternates (Rising / Peak / Trough / Steady lavender + sage) swap with the in-app theme, custom hues map to the nearest theme; explicit dark variants; one stealth alt.
  • Principle — discretion: the icon never outs a user as being on HRT at a glance. The name itself is the first discretion asset.
  • Carried forward — landing headline direction: “Shaped protocol.” (sketch still under the old byTime name).
