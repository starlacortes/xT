## 1. Articles of Organization (Ohio Form 533A)

File online at **Ohio Business Central** (https://business.ohio.gov) — same-day
processing. The paper Form 533A is only needed if you mail it; the online form
asks the same questions. Field-by-field content:

| Form field | Entry |
|---|---|
| **Article 1 — Name** | `Isocline Labs LLC` |
| **Article 2 — Effective date** | Leave blank (effective on filing date) |
| **Article 3 — Period of existence** | Leave blank (defaults to perpetual) |
| **Article 4 — Purpose** | `Any lawful purpose` |
| **Article 5 — Statutory agent** | Your full legal name, plus your Ohio street address (P.O. box is **not** acceptable; must be a physical street address in Ohio). |
| **Article 6 — Additional provisions** | Leave blank (or add indemnification language if your attorney advises — not required) |
| **Signatory** | Your name and signature as authorized representative |

### Statutory agent notes

- The agent must maintain a physical presence at the listed Ohio address during
  business hours to accept service of process.
- **Your name and address become public record** on the Ohio SOS business search.
  If you do not want your home address public, use a commercial registered-agent
  service instead (that was the other option in the earlier question).
- Ohio does not require a separate "agent acceptance" form when the agent is the
  same person signing the Articles.

## 2. Operating Agreement

Ohio law requires an operating agreement (it is not filed with the state, but
you must have one and keep it with your records). A full single-member template
is in `Isocline-Labs-LLC-Operating-Agreement.md`. Sign it yourself — as
single-member you sign both as "Member" and as "Manager."

Why it matters even though you are the only owner: it is the document that
proves the LLC is a real, separate entity and not your personal alter ego. That
separation is what keeps personal assets protected and is what a bank or
investor will ask to see.

## 3. EIN (IRS Form SS-4) — free, online

Apply at **https://www.irs.gov/ein** (the online assistant issues the number
instantly during business hours).

| SS-4 field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Legal name | `Isocline Labs LLC` |
| Entity type | Limited liability company |
| Number of members | 1 (this makes it a "disregarded entity" for federal tax — profits/losses flow to your personal return, no separate LLC return) |
| Reason for applying | "Started a new business" |
| Responsible party | Your name + SSN (the IRS requires a natural person here, not the LLC) |

Note: a single-member LLC with no employees does **not** strictly need an EIN —
you can use your SSN — but an EIN is free, keeps your SSN off vendor/bank forms,
and most banks require one to open a business account. Get it.

## 4. Post-formation checklist (in order)

- [ ] File Articles of Organization ($99) — save the confirmation/certificate.
- [ ] Sign and keep the operating agreement.
- [ ] Apply for EIN (free).
- [ ] Open a **business bank account** in the LLC's name (bring Articles + EIN +
      operating agreement). Do not mix personal and business funds from day one.
- [ ] Register for Ohio **Commercial Activity Tax (CAT)** only if/when taxable
      gross receipts exceed $150,000/year — not relevant at launch. No other
      Ohio LLC tax registration is automatic.
- [ ] **Update the trademark worksheet** (`TRADEMARK_APPLICATION.md`): the
      "Owner of Mark" is now the exact string `Isocline Labs LLC`, organized in
      Ohio. This unblocks the TEAS Plus filing.
- [ ] File the trademark (TEAS Plus, Class 9, §1(b)) once the LLC is formed and
      the class 9 clearance search is done.
- [ ] Enroll in the **App Store Small Business Program** and align it with the
      LLC + developer account (see `MONETIZATION_AND_LAUNCH_STRATEGY.md` §84).

## 5. What NOT to worry about yet

- **No Ohio annual report fee** — Ohio LLCs pay no annual report; there is
  nothing due every year at the state level.
- **No publication requirement** — Ohio does not require newspaper publication
  (unlike NY/NE).
- **No registered-agent acceptance filing** when you are your own agent.
